## Rights policy
- Government and open data are not an excuse to use agencies' trademarks or any third-party content embedded in a government portal.
- Copyrightable full text, photos, video, audio, logo and broadcaster names are not imported as news assets.
- Every record carries a source label and canonical HTTPS source link. Aggregation does **not** imply government endorsement.
- If license rights are unclear, do not enable the provider, and do not republish its headline or summary.
- Permission records should include author/organization, scope, location, date, expiration and copy of signed authorization.
- These notes are engineering controls, not a legal guarantee that no party can ever assert a claim.
